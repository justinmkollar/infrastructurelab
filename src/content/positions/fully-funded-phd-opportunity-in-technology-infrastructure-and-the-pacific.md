description: <p>PhD opportunity with the Infrastructure Lab at the National
  University of Singapore for students interested in how technology industries
  reshape cities and regions through infrastructure, land, energy, and resource
  systems. The geographic focus is broadly trans-Pacific, while more focused
  national, regional, or local projects are also welcome.</p>
intro: Fully funded PhD opportunity for the August 2027 intake in the Department
  of Architecture, College of Design and Engineering, National University of
  Singapore.
modules:
  - type: text
    heading: About
    body: <p>I am seeking a prospective PhD student to join the Infrastructure Lab
      at the National University of Singapore. The position is intended for a
      researcher interested in how technology industries reshape cities,
      regions, and territories through infrastructure development, industrial
      policy, and the use of land, energy, and other resources.</p><p>The
      research agenda is broadly trans-Pacific, with particular interests in
      East and Southeast Asia and the United States. Comparative projects
      examining connections between these regions are welcome, as are focused
      investigations of particular national, regional, or local
      contexts.</p><p>The successful candidate will pursue a PhD by Research in
      the NUS Department of Architecture under my supervision. They will develop
      an independent research project while participating in the Lab's broader
      intellectual and research activities.</p>
  - type: text
    heading: Research
    body: <p>Possible research directions include data centers and semiconductor
      production, industrial and infrastructure policy, energy systems,
      environmental governance, and the changing geographies of technology-led
      development.</p><p>These are not fixed topics. The aim is to support a
      student in developing their own research questions within this broader
      agenda. Research may involve fieldwork, interviews, archival and policy
      analysis, GIS, or other qualitative, quantitative, and spatial
      methods.</p>
  - type: text
    heading: Qualifications
    body: <p>Applicants should have a strong academic background in urban planning,
      geography, public policy, urban studies, political economy, architecture,
      or a related field. I’m particularly interested in candidates who
      demonstrate:</p><ul><li><p>Strong research and academic writing
      skills.</p></li><li><p>Experience conducting independent research, ideally
      through a thesis, publication, or comparable project.</p></li><li><p>An
      interest in empirical research and the ability to develop original
      research questions.</p></li><li><p>Familiarity with relevant qualitative,
      quantitative, or spatial methods.</p></li></ul><p>A master's degree is
      preferred, although applicants with strong undergraduate qualifications
      may also be considered in accordance with NUS admission requirements.
      Prior publications are welcome but not required.</p>
  - type: text
    heading: Funding and research environment
    body: <p>The position is intended to be fully funded through the NUS Research
      Scholarship, subject to admission and scholarship approval. The
      scholarship covers tuition fees and provides a monthly stipend for up to
      four years, subject to satisfactory academic progress. Stipend rates and
      other conditions vary by residency status and are detailed on the <a
      href="https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/nus-research-scholarship/">NUS
      Research Scholarship website</a>.</p><p>The successful candidate will
      pursue an independent PhD project under my supervision while participating
      in the Infrastructure Lab's research activities. Students will be
      encouraged to publish their research, present at academic conferences, and
      engage with the wider research community.</p>
  - type: text
    heading: Expression of interest
    body: <p>Interested applicants are encouraged to contact me before submitting a
      formal application. Please email <a
      href="mailto:jkollar@nus.edu.sg"><strong>jkollar@nus.edu.sg</strong></a>
      with:</p><ol><li><p>An academic CV.</p></li><li><p>A short research
      statement (1–2 pages) outlining your interests, potential research
      questions, and relevant experience.</p></li><li><p>A writing sample, such
      as a thesis chapter, research paper, or
      publication.</p></li></ol><p>Promising candidates may be invited for an
      informal discussion about their research interests and fit with the
      Lab.</p><p>Formal applications must be submitted separately through NUS.
      The main application deadline for the August 2027 intake is <strong>15
      January 2027</strong>.</p><p>Further information:</p><ul><li><p><a
      href="https://cde.nus.edu.sg/arch/admission/maarchbyresearch-phdbyresearch/">PhD
