abstract: "<p>The planned road to Myanmar's Dawei Special Economic Zone would cut through the forested Tanintharyi region near the Thai border, an area important for biodiversity and local livelihoods. This case study examines the environmental uncertainties surrounding that transport project and the use of spatial models to estimate its effects on forest connectivity and wildlife habitat. It considers how infrastructure plans, conservation priorities, and limited local information complicate decisions about development. The study illustrates how scenario-based analysis can bring ecological consequences into infrastructure planning before construction begins.</p>"
