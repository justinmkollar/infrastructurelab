It examines how state agencies depend on major domestic firms and global supply chains to advance infrastructure strategies. The cases of Thailand's Eastern Economic Corridor and Taiwan's semiconductor industry show how industrial and geopolitical projects emerge through negotiations among governments, domestic corporations, and international investors.</p>"
