abstract: "<p>This article examines Oregon's data center and semiconductor expansion as a process that reorganizes territorial governance and resource systems. Focusing on Amazon, Google, and Intel, it traces the fiscal incentives, utility arrangements, and land use instruments used to secure large industrial electricity loads. Case studies in The Dalles, Morrow/Umatilla, and Hillsboro show how contractual, regulatory, and executive decision-making can accelerate development while shifting environmental and fiscal costs onto municipalities and regional ecosystems.</p>"
