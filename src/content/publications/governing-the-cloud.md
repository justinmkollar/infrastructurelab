abstract: <p>This article examines Oregon’s data-center and semiconductor
  expansion as a process that reorganizes territorial governance and resource
  systems. Focusing on firms including Amazon, Google, and Intel, it traces the
  fiscal incentives, utility arrangements, and land-use instruments used to
  secure large industrial electricity loads. The analysis shows how important
  decisions increasingly move into contractual, regulatory, and executive
  venues, accelerating infrastructure deployment while redistributing
  environmental and fiscal consequences across municipalities, utilities, and
  regional ecosystems.</p>
