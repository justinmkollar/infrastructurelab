abstract: "<p>This article investigates how Taiwan's semiconductor expansion reorganizes the allocation and governance of water across industrial and agricultural landscapes. Drawing on fieldwork, spatial analysis, and policy research, it examines the institutions and infrastructure that prioritize industrial supply through irrigation fallowing, interbasin transfers, and water recycling. It argues that semiconductor development depends on territorial restructuring that redistributes environmental risks and access to resources beyond factory sites.</p>"
