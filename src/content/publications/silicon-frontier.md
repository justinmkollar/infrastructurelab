abstract: <p>Silicon Frontier investigates how semiconductor manufacturing, cloud infrastructure, and AI computing are transforming land, water, energy systems, and development priorities across the Pacific Basin. The book develops the concept of techno-statecraft to explain how states, firms, utilities, and local governments assemble infrastructure as industrial strategy. Through comparative research in Taiwan, the United States, China, and Southeast Asia, it connects geopolitical competition over advanced technology to the territorial institutions and socio-ecological systems that make technological expansion possible.</p>
