Drawing on local regulatory conflicts and state legislation between 2024 and 2026, it examines how planning authority is contested and reorganized during infrastructure expansion. It argues for improved municipal regulation alongside regional and state mechanisms for disclosure, infrastructure coordination, and democratic accountability.</p>"
