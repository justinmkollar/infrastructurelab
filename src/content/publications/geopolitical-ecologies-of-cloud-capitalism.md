abstract: "<p>This article examines the territorial foundations of national computing capacity in the United States and China. It argues that geopolitical ambitions for artificial intelligence and cloud computing depend on the domestic reorganization of land, electricity, infrastructure, and regulatory authority. By situating data center expansion within regional development and environmental governance, the comparison shows how ostensibly national technological strategies are assembled through specific places and material resource systems.</p>"
