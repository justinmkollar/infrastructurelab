abstract: "<p>This article analyzes how the expansion of artificial intelligence computing supports investment in fossil fuel generation, transmission, and power-intensive industrial sites. Comparing developments associated with PJM and ERCOT, it introduces powered land as an asset assembled through electrical deliverability, revenue guarantees, and cost-allocation arrangements. It argues that fossil energy, finance, and technology firms convert uncertain computing demand into long-duration infrastructure commitments while shifting costs and limiting public scrutiny.</p>"
