abstract: "<p>This chapter examines Hong Kong's changing water supply infrastructure, from local reservoirs to the interregional system that conveys water from mainland China. It considers how hydraulic engineering, colonial histories, and cross-border agreements have shaped the territory's water security. By tracing material networks and political relationships, it shows that the provision of urban water depends on infrastructure extending beyond Hong Kong's administrative boundaries.</p>"
