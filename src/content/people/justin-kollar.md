bio: <p>Justin Kollar is a planner and urban designer whose research examines
  how infrastructure, environmental change, and territorial governance shape
  contemporary urbanization. His work develops the concept of techno-statecraft
  to study how semiconductor fabs, data centers, energy systems, and other
  high-technology infrastructures reorganize land, water, and development
  priorities. He holds a PhD in Urban and Regional Planning from MIT and
  master’s degrees in architecture and urban planning from Harvard Graduate
  School of Design. His comparative research focuses on the United States and
  Asia.</p>
